Crypto Breakout Scanner: Find Live Breakout Candidates

A breakout scanner looks for markets moving beyond recent structure. Crypto Confluence Radar adds breakout evidence to a broader technical score.

How breakout detection works

The engine compares price with recent highs and evaluates EMA direction, MACD momentum, RSI and VWAP. This distinguishes a simple new high from a move supported by several independent observations.

False breakouts

Crypto markets frequently trade above resistance and return below it. No scanner can eliminate that risk. Closing price, volume behavior and higher-timeframe levels provide additional context.
