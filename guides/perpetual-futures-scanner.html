What PerpMomentum scans

The live terminal focuses on Binance USDT perpetual futures and displays qualifying markets in a ranked technical flow. Selecting a market opens its daily chart for visual inspection.

Why a ranking is useful

A ranking turns a large market universe into a smaller research shortlist. The 24-hour count emphasizes markets that repeatedly meet the scanner condition rather than treating every occurrence as identical.

Scanner versus signal

A scanner identifies conditions. It does not know a trader's risk tolerance, position size or invalidation point, so its output should be treated as research rather than an instruction to enter a position.
