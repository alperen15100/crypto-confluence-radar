Crypto VWAP Scanner: Volume-Weighted Market Context

VWAP summarizes average traded price weighted by volume over the analyzed candle set. The radar uses it as market context rather than a guaranteed support or resistance line.

Above and below VWAP

Price above VWAP indicates trading above its volume-weighted reference; below VWAP indicates the opposite. Meaning depends on timeframe, trend and structure.

VWAP plus confluence

VWAP becomes more informative near a volume-profile point of control, support, fair value gap or previous-session level. The scanner rewards agreement among independent evidence.
