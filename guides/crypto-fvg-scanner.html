Crypto FVG Scanner: Fair Value Gap Detection

The FVG scanner searches recent candles for three-candle price imbalances and uses them as one component of broader confluence analysis.

What is a fair value gap?

A bullish gap can appear when the low of a later candle remains above the high two candles earlier. A bearish gap is the inverse condition. These zones describe rapid price movement with relatively little overlap.

FVG is context, not certainty

A gap does not prove that price will revisit or react. The radar compares FVG evidence with ATR distance, trend, VWAP, RSI, support, resistance and volume-profile levels.
