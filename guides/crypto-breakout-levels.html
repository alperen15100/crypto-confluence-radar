Crypto Breakout Levels

A horizontal level gives momentum a visual price-structure reference on the daily chart.

What is a breakout level?

A breakout level is a prior price reference that the market is testing or moving beyond. It can help distinguish momentum occurring inside an old range from momentum pushing into a new area.

Why use a daily chart?

Daily candles reduce some of the noise visible on lower timeframes and make broader horizontal structure easier to inspect. PerpMomentum keeps the chart at 1D for this reason.

Do breakouts always continue?

No. Breakouts can fail, reverse or retest. The displayed level is context for research, not a prediction or guarantee.
