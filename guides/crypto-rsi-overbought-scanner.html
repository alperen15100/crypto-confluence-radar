What does RSI 70 mean?

The Relative Strength Index is a momentum oscillator. A reading at or above 70 is commonly described as overbought, but it does not guarantee an immediate reversal. Strong trends can remain overbought for extended periods.

Why rank repeated overbought conditions?

A single threshold touch and a market that repeatedly remains above the threshold describe different momentum persistence. PerpMomentum keeps a rolling 24-hour occurrence count so markets can be ranked by repeated qualifying scans.

Use price structure as context

Momentum is easier to interpret when viewed beside the daily chart and a horizontal breakout reference. The scanner therefore combines the ranking with a 1D chart rather than presenting RSI as a standalone trade instruction.
