Crypto RSI Scanner: Find Overbought & Oversold Markets

An RSI scanner compares momentum across many crypto markets without opening every chart manually. Crypto Confluence Radar evaluates RSI alongside trend, volume and market structure rather than treating one oscillator as a standalone signal.

What does RSI measure?

The Relative Strength Index is a momentum oscillator on a 0–100 scale. Readings near or below 30 can highlight unusually weak momentum, while readings near or above 70 can highlight unusually strong momentum. Neither level automatically means price must reverse.

Why confluence matters

An oversold RSI near support, a previous-week low or a bullish fair value gap has different context from oversold RSI during a strong breakdown. The radar combines RSI with EMA trend, VWAP, MACD, volume profile and nearby structure.

How to use it

Open the Oversold or Overbought radar, compare confluence scores, then inspect the chart and key levels. Treat the result as a research shortlist, not an entry instruction.
