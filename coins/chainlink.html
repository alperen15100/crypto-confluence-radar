Chainlink confluence checklist

Start with EMA trend direction. Compare momentum with RSI and MACD, then check whether LINK trades above or below VWAP. Finally inspect support, resistance, volume-profile POC, fair value gaps and previous-day or previous-week levels.

What makes stronger confluence?

Independent signals agreeing near the same price area are more informative than several versions of the same indicator. A structural level plus volume context plus improving momentum provides broader evidence than momentum alone.
