How the Confluence Score Works

The radar ranks technical conditions; it does not forecast guaranteed returns. The score starts from a neutral baseline and adjusts as independent observations agree or conflict.

Signal families

Trend: EMA relationships. Momentum: RSI and MACD. Volume context: VWAP and volume-profile POC. Structure: support, resistance, breakouts, fair value gaps and previous-day or previous-week levels.

Limitations

Indicators use historical market data and can lag or fail. Candle-based volume profile is an approximation. A high score is not a probability of profit.
