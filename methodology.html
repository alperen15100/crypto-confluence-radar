How PerpMomentum Works

PerpMomentum is a browser-based research scanner for USDT perpetual futures. It is designed to surface persistent daily momentum and place it beside price structure.

Market universe

The scanner loads trading USDT perpetual futures markets and evaluates their daily candle data.

Momentum filter

Markets must meet the scanner's daily RSI overbought condition and its price-history qualification before appearing in the technical flow.

24-hour ranking

Each qualifying scan is recorded in a rolling 24-hour window. Markets with more qualifying occurrences rank above markets with fewer occurrences.

Daily chart and breakout level

Selecting a ranked market opens a 1D candlestick chart and displays a horizontal breakout reference to provide visual structure context.

Limitations

RSI and horizontal levels are descriptive technical tools, not forecasts. Market data can be delayed or temporarily unavailable, and historical patterns do not guarantee future outcomes.

PerpMomentum is an educational market-research tool by Ecrin Labs. Nothing on the site is financial advice.
